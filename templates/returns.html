RETURNS & EXCHANGES

3-Day Returns & Exchange Policy

Customers may request a return or exchange within 3 days of receiving an item.

Items must be unworn, unused, unwashed, undamaged and returned in their original condition with any tags and packaging.

Items showing signs of wear, damage, alteration, washing, perfume, makeup or other use may not be accepted.

If you receive an incorrect or defective item, contact RUTHELYN COLLECTIONS as soon as possible and provide clear photos where requested.

Return or exchange delivery costs may apply unless the item received was incorrect or defective.

To request a return or exchange, WhatsApp 0812 550 6022 or call 0815 398 4064.
